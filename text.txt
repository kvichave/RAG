Agentic AI refers to autonomous software systems that use large language models as a cognitive core to perceive environments, reason through complex problems, plan multi-step actions, and execute tasks with minimal human intervention. Unlike traditional generative AI, which stops at answering prompts or generating content, agentic AI actively interacts with external tools, APIs, and databases to achieve specific, high-level goals. Through a continuous loop of perception, action, reflection, and adaptation, these systems can autonomously troubleshoot, coordinate multi-agent workflows, and drive efficiency across industries like software development, finance, and customer support.
